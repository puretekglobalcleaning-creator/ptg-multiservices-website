How to read these

What most credit companies won't tell you

If you have been burned before, you have probably been shown numbers that quietly left something out. Here is what is behind ours.

01

Progress is not monthly

Keyun's file showed zero movement on all three bureaus one month this summer. Judged on that month alone you would think nothing was happening. His total is +94, +71 and +89. Credit work shows up over the arc of a file, not in any single 30-day window.

02

Not every client is on this page

Some of our clients' scores went down this cycle — usually because a new collection landed or a positive account fell off. We tell those clients the truth in writing, and we do not put their file on a marketing page.

03

A deletion is not always a win

When a positive account drops off a report, the count of "items removed" goes up and the client's score goes down. We itemize what actually came off — creditor, amount and bureau — instead of quoting you a number.
