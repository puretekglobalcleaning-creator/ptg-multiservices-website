Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026

Who We Are

PTG Multiservices is a trade name of Puretek Global LLC, a Texas limited liability company located in Houston, Texas. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Puretek Global LLC. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you give us directly when you request a consultation, become a client, or contact us:

  • Contact information — your name, email address, mailing address, and mobile or landline phone number.
  • Identity information — date of birth and the last four digits of your Social Security number, where required to request your consumer reports on your behalf.
  • Consumer credit information — the contents of your credit reports from the nationwide consumer reporting agencies, obtained with your written authorization through a credit monitoring service you enroll in.
  • Correspondence — the messages you send us and the records of disputes we file on your behalf.

We also collect limited technical information automatically when you visit this website, such as your browser type and the pages you view.

How We Use Your Information

  • To evaluate your credit file and prepare and submit disputes on your behalf.
  • To communicate with you about your file — when a round is mailed, when results come back, and what changed.
  • To schedule and conduct consultations.
  • To send you documents, agreements, and billing communications.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory, and recordkeeping obligations.

Mobile Information and Text Messaging

No mobile information will be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party, and are not sold, rented, or shared for any purpose other than delivering the messages you asked to receive.

Information may be shared only with the service providers strictly necessary to deliver our own messages to you — for example, our messaging carrier — and those providers are prohibited from using it for their own purposes.

See our SMS Terms for the full messaging program details, including how to stop messages at any time.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these circumstances:

  • Consumer reporting agencies and furnishers — we send disputes and supporting documentation on your behalf, as you authorize.
  • Service providers — the credit-repair platform, credit monitoring service, e-signature, payment, email, and messaging providers we use to run our business. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Regulators and legal process — when required by law, subpoena, or to respond to a government request.
  • With your direction — when you ask us to share information with a lender, broker, or other party.

Your Rights

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to the records we are required to retain. You may cancel our services at any time. Contact us using the details below and we will respond promptly.

You have rights under the federal Credit Repair Organizations Act, including the right to cancel your contract with us without penalty or obligation within three business days of signing it, and the right to dispute inaccurate information on your credit report yourself, at no cost, by contacting the consumer reporting agencies directly.

Data Retention and Security

We keep client records for as long as your file is active, and afterward for the period our recordkeeping obligations require. We use commercially reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cookies

This website uses only the cookies necessary for the site and its embedded consultation form to function. We do not use this site to build advertising profiles.

Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent version.
